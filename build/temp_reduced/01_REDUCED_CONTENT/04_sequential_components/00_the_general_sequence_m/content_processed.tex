\section{The General Sequence Model in Python}

Python's \emph{sequence} abstraction covers objects whose components occupy definite positions. Strings, lists, and tuples share a positional access surface---indexing via \texttt{\_\_getitem\_\_}, length via \texttt{\_\_len\_\_}, membership via \texttt{\_\_contains\_\_}---but they partition into mutable (\texttt{list}) and immutable (\texttt{str}, \texttt{tuple}) families. The shared model is the entry point; the sections below expose where that uniformity ends at the heap.
