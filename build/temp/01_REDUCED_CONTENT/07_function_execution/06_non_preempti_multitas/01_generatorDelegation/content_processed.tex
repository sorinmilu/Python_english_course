\subsection{Generator Delegation}

\texttt{yield from} delegates iteration to a sub-generator or iterable, forwarding sent values and propagated exceptions through the chain.

\subsubsection{\texttt{yield from} as Delegated Iteration over Subgenerators and Iterables}

\begin{codebox}[]{python}{yield from as Delegated Iteration over Subgenerators and Iterables}{gen01}
def inner():
    yield 1
    yield 2

def outer():
    yield from inner()
    yield 3

print(list(outer()))
\end{codebox}


\begin{iobox}{Expected Output}
[1, 2, 3] \\
\end{iobox}




The outer generator suspends while the inner generator runs, sharing the delegation protocol without manually iterating.

\subsubsection{Propagating Values, Exceptions, and Completion Through Delegated Generator Chains}

\texttt{yield from sub} forwards \texttt{send()}, \texttt{throw()}, and \texttt{close()} to the sub-generator. The completion value of the sub-generator becomes the result of the \texttt{yield from} expression. Exception propagation follows the generator protocol stack.
